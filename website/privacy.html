Privacy notice

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, nothing sold. The website keeps web server logs for 14 days; the game server keeps your Minecraft account and your world. Nothing is shared with anyone except the company that hosts us.

Who we are

HoneyBeeSMP is a small, volunteer-run Minecraft community. There is no company behind it, nothing here is sold, and there is no commercial activity of any kind. Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, the community is the controller of the limited personal data described on this page. You can reach us at admin@honeybeesmp.co.uk, or in the Discord server, which is usually faster.

What this notice covers

This website, the Minecraft server it describes, and the Discord community attached to it. It does not cover Discord itself, Modrinth, or Mojang and Microsoft. Each of those runs its own service and has its own privacy policy.

The website

Like any web server, ours records each request in a log file. An entry holds your IP address, the date and time, the address you asked for, the response code, the number of bytes sent, the page you came from and your browser's user agent. That is the only personal data the website produces.

We use it to keep the site running and to keep it secure — diagnosing faults and spotting abuse. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the service. We do not use it to build a profile, and it is not combined with anything else. Logs rotate daily and are kept for 14 days, after which they are deleted.

The server is hosted by Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, which processes these logs on our behalf as our hosting provider. Nobody else sees them.

The live map at map.honeybeesmp.co.uk is served by the same web server, and its requests appear in the same log with the same retention.

The site loads nothing from third parties. There are no web fonts, no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no social embeds and no CDNs — every file you see came from our server. The only things that leave are ordinary links to Discord and Modrinth, and a link is not an embed: nothing is loaded from them until you choose to follow one.

The Minecraft server

When you join the server we process:

  • your Minecraft username and the UUID Mojang gives you, which identify your player;
  • your IP address while you are connected;
  • your player data and the world — your builds, inventories, claims and parties;
  • server logs, which record connections, commands and in-game chat.

We use these to run the game: to authenticate you, to keep your world intact, to moderate the community, and to prevent griefing and abuse. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating a multiplayer server you have chosen to join.

Two things worth knowing. First, chat is not reported to Mojang, because NoChatReports is installed, but the server itself still sees your messages and may record them in its logs. Second, if you are removed for breaking the rules, we keep a record of that so the ban can be enforced.

World data and your account record are kept for as long as the server runs and you remain a member. Server logs rotate on the same schedule as the web logs. We share none of this, except with our hosting provider. Mojang and Microsoft handle your login and never pass your credentials to us: they know you signed in, and we see only your username and UUID.

Discord

The Discord server runs on Discord Inc.'s platform, so their privacy policy governs what happens there. We see what any member of a Discord sees: your display name, the messages you post in channels we can read, and when you join or leave. Our bot uses that to assign roles and to lay out the channels and messages the server is set up with. It does not keep a database of members and stores no personal data of its own.

What we never do

  • sell, rent or trade your data;
  • use it for advertising, retargeting or profiling;
  • run analytics or tracking on this website;
  • share it with anyone other than our hosting provider.

How long we keep things

  • Web server logs — 14 days, then deleted.
  • Minecraft account and world data — while you are a member.
  • Ban records — for as long as the ban is in force.
  • Discord — we keep nothing of our own; Discord holds it.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to how we use it, or ask for a copy in a portable form. Where we rely on legitimate interests you have the right to object, and we will stop unless we have a compelling reason to continue.

Because we hold so little, most requests are quick. Ask in the Discord and we will deal with it within one month, which is the legal deadline. There is no automated decision-making or profiling here.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We are in Scotland; the ICO covers the whole of the UK. We would rather you came to us first.

Children

Minecraft's own terms set a minimum age of 13, and this server is aimed at people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is happy for you to play. We do not knowingly collect anything beyond what is described here from anyone. If you believe a child's data has ended up with us and you want it removed, tell us in the Discord and we will remove it.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Anything material will be announced in the Discord.

Contact

Email admin@honeybeesmp.co.uk, or ask in the Discord server. Use the email for anything formal, such as a data request; the Discord is faster for ordinary questions.
