Cookie policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version. This website sets no cookies at all. There is nothing to opt in to, and no tracking to switch off.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website asks your browser to store. Related technologies, such as localStorage, do a similar job from inside the browser. They are useful for things like keeping you signed in — and they are also the usual mechanism for tracking people from one site to the next.

Does this site use cookies?

No. There are no session cookies, no login, no analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Matomo, nothing like them), no advertising or retargeting pixels and no social media embeds.

You can check this yourself: the server sends no Set-Cookie header on any page. Everything the site needs is in the files you download.

Because there is nothing non-essential to consent to, no consent gate is required under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. The notice you saw exists to tell you that, not to make you click through a formality.

The live map at map.honeybeesmp.co.uk is part of the same service and does not set cookies either.

What is stored in your browser

One thing, and only once you dismiss the notice: an entry in your browser's localStorage named honeybee.cookie.notice, with the value dismissed.

Its only purpose is to stop the notice appearing again on every page. It contains no identifier, no timestamp and nothing about you, and it never reaches us — you can read it yourself in your browser's developer tools.

Because it exists solely to honour your choice, and stores nothing about you, it falls within the strictly necessary exemption and does not require consent. Clearing your browser's site data removes it, and the notice will simply appear again.

Server logs are not cookies

Our web server records requests in a log file, which is described in the privacy notice. That is a record on our server, not something stored on your device.

Other sites we link to

Following a link to Discord or Modrinth takes you to their site, where their own cookies and policies apply. Those are set by them, not by us, and we have no control over them. Nothing is loaded from either site while you are here.

How to control what is stored

Every browser lets you see and delete the data sites have stored, and block it in future — usually under Settings, then Privacy. Doing so will not break this website; the only effect is that the cookie notice keeps coming back.

If we ever add something that does need consent — analytics, say — we will ask before it loads, and this page and the notice will change first.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions can go to admin@honeybeesmp.co.uk or the Discord server.
